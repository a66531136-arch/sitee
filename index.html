Before you message me

What does it cost?

Pricing is agreed on a call after I understand the clinic and scope. My fee, ad spend and any additional services are separate. The test is paid; all amounts and the terms for continuing or stopping are agreed before launch.

Do you guarantee bookings or revenue?

No fixed number of bookings or revenue is promised. My role is campaign management and improving the relevance of inquiries against agreed criteria. Bookings also depend on your administrator, pricing, availability and patient decisions.

Can we start without a website or CRM?

Yes. If appropriate for the clinic and market, ads can direct people to Instagram messages or WhatsApp. We choose the route during the audit. A new website is an optional, separately priced project.

Who answers patient inquiries?

Your administrator. I can provide a message script and review the handling of inquiries using the access we agree on. A bot is an optional separate service, not part of basic ad management.

Who makes the ad images and videos?

Your designer, social media team or another specialist. I prepare the brief and review materials for the campaign. I do not provide graphic design or video editing as part of the ad service.

What happens if the test underperforms?

I share the results openly and explain what was tested. We agree whether to stop or run another stage. Continuation requires a joint decision. Payment and stopping terms are agreed before launch; a break-even result is not guaranteed.

Who owns the accounts and campaign materials?

We primarily work in your advertising accounts with the access needed for the project. At the end you retain your accounts, commissioned materials and agreed project assets. Any additional setup costs are agreed in advance.

Will these results apply to my market?

The medical example uses KZT and comes from my previous work. It shows an approach and a recorded result. Outcomes depend on the local market, clinic and offer, so we test your campaign separately.

Which medical projects do you take on?

Medical and dental practices, rehabilitation and treatment-focused dermatology. I do not take projects promoting cosmetology services. We confirm the fit during the initial conversation.

Can I speak to a previous client?

The portfolio links to public project pages where available. A personal introduction can be considered after work begins and only with the former client’s permission. I do not share private contacts without consent.
